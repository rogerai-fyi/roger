Local inference where the network stops.

Control networks are segmented below a DMZ and do not route to the internet. That is not an obstacle to work around - it is the reason a local model is worth having, and it decides everything about where one is allowed to run.

§1 / MARKETS

Four places the constraint is the point.

Deployment patterns, not customer case studies. Models remain advisory and stay out of real-time closed-loop control.

Oil and gas

Rotating-equipment anomaly explanation and operator copilots grounded in the OEM manuals and P&IDs already on site.

Power generation

Historian questions in plain language, alarm-flood triage, and work-order drafting for turbines and substation assets.

Manufacturing

Prompt-defined inspection over the existing classifier, and shift notes, maintenance logs, and fault codes read together.

Aerospace and defense

Document intelligence and assistant workloads on disconnected networks, where deployment is the security model.

Signals. OPC UA and Modbus for tags; MQTT Sparkplug B over a unified namespace for transport.

Models. ISA-95 for the asset hierarchy and NAMUR NE 107 for device-health status, so a verdict lands in the vocabulary operators already use.

Placement. IEC 62443 zones and the Purdue levels, drawn above. The model reasons beside classical analytics, never in place of it.

Device evidence contract. A device claim names the exact board, runtime, format, quantization, context, peak memory, and measured speed.

Raspberry Pi and NPU. Pi results name model and RAM. NPU results report full delegation, partial delegation, and CPU fallback, not merely a successful load.

ESP32. Roger Edge covers wake, VAD, sensing, and fixed commands. ESP32 does not run Wave Micro; ESP32-P4 claims name the companion radio.

§2 / THE BOUNDARY

What it does not do.

Worth stating plainly, because the failure mode of an advisory system is someone trusting it past its remit.

No closed-loop control. The model reads and explains. Protection, interlocks and safety-instrumented systems remain deterministic and are not in its reach.

No replacement for classical analytics. Residual-based anomaly detection on high-rate vibration and thermodynamic data is a signal-processing problem, and it stays one. A language model sits on top as a reasoning layer, explaining an alarm rather than raising it.

No claim without a measurement. A device claim names the exact board, runtime, format, quantization, context, peak memory, and measured speed - or it is not made. The evidence rules apply here exactly as they do to a model card.
