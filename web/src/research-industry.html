Control networks are segmented below a DMZ and do not route to the internet. A local model fits that design, and the placement follows from it.

§1 / MARKETS

Eight industries whose data cannot leave the site.

A cloud model is ruled out here. Three things rule it out: segmented networks, hazardous areas, and links too thin to stream sensor data. That is the constraint we build for. The engagements are under NDA and not named here. In every deployment the model advises a person and stays outside the control loop: it never actuates equipment, and it is never part of a protection or safety path.

Oil and gas

Rotating-equipment anomaly explanation and operator copilots grounded in the OEM manuals and P&IDs already on site.

Power generation

Historian questions in plain language, alarm-flood triage, and work-order drafting for turbines and substation assets.

Manufacturing

Prompt-defined inspection over the existing classifier, and shift notes, maintenance logs, and fault codes read together.

Aerospace

Document intelligence and assistant workloads on disconnected networks, where deployment is the security model.

Mining and metals

Fixed-plant and haul-fleet condition explanation, with shift and geology logs read together at sites a link barely reaches.

Water and wastewater

Treatment-process questions against the local historian, and permit and compliance drafting on a network that does not route out.

Healthcare

The equipment layer of a hospital: imaging-fleet service logs, biomedical work orders, alarm-configuration review, and cold-chain records - on the segmented networks that data cannot leave.

Defense sustainment

Maintenance, readiness, and supply on denied, degraded, and intermittent links, where there is no reachback to a cloud.

Healthcare: equipment and facilities, not patients. The models read the machines, the logs, and the paperwork. They do not diagnose, do not treat, do not recommend or adjust care, and do not read scans or physiologic waveforms. Alarm work is offline review of alarm journals to find misconfiguration - never a decision about a live alarm at a bedside. It is the same no-actuation contract that keeps a model out of a plant’s control loop, applied to a hospital.

Defense: sustainment, not effects. Maintenance, readiness, and supply. No targeting, no weapons employment, and no intelligence exploitation.

Signals. OPC UA and Modbus for tags; MQTT Sparkplug B over a unified namespace for transport.

Models. ISA-95 for the asset hierarchy and NAMUR NE 107 for device-health status, so a verdict lands in the vocabulary operators already use.

Placement. IEC 62443 zones and the Purdue levels, drawn above. The model reasons beside classical analytics, never in place of it.

Device evidence contract. A device claim names the exact board, runtime, format, quantization, context, peak memory, and measured speed.

Raspberry Pi and NPU. Pi results name model and RAM. NPU results report full delegation, partial delegation, and CPU fallback, not merely a successful load.

ESP32. Roger Edge is the sensing and glue layer: wake, VAD, sensing, and fixed commands. No Wave tier runs on an ESP32 - the board is the sensor and a Raspberry Pi is the model host. ESP32-P4 claims name the companion radio.

§2 / THE ENVELOPE

The tiers are not a preference. They are what certification allows.

Before anyone talks architecture, three practical questions settle where a model can physically run.

Heat. Temperature class T4 caps surface temperature at 135°C at maximum ambient, and oil and gas sites routinely ask for T3 or T4. In a sealed fanless enclosure at 50 to 60°C ambient that is the constraint that kills compute, long before anyone runs out of TOPS.

Power in the zone. Intrinsic safety (Ex ia) limits usable power to roughly 1.2 to 1.3 watts. That is a transmitter or a microcontroller, not an accelerator. We have not found a hazardous-area-certified GPU enclosure on the market. The shapes that do work are a safe-area rack, a purged cabinet, light inference on a certified low-power gateway, or sensors in the zone with the compute outside on fiber.

Power at a remote site. Remote sites size three to seven days of battery autonomy. Every continuous watt costs roughly a 10 to 15 watt panel and 30 to 60 watt-hours of battery, so a 15 watt device is a 200 to 300 watt array and a 2 to 4 kilowatt-hour bank - a skid of its own. Duty-cycled inference or microcontroller-class compute is the only thing that fits a solar wellhead.
