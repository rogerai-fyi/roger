About the receipt chain

Every request your station serves produces a signed receipt, and each receipt links to the one before it. The network records where your station's chain was, so a gap is visible rather than silent.

A break is an audit signal, not a penalty. Restarting your station starts a new chain, which reads as a break today - that is expected and does not affect your earnings or standing.
