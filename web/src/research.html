§2 / PROOF

Claims follow measurements.

Compact pages do not mean hidden evidence.

Reproducible. Published measurements name the hardware, artifact, runtime, settings, and raw results.

§3 / DEPLOYMENT

Local inference where the network stops.

Oil and gas, power generation, manufacturing, aerospace, mining, and water utilities: plants and field sites run segmented or air-gapped networks, and that constraint is the whole opportunity. Models stay advisory and out of real-time closed-loop control.

§5 / DEVELOPERS

From artifact to local endpoint.

Released model cards carry exact commands. The flow stays the same across the family.

01

Download

Pull a released artifact and its model card from Hugging Face.

huggingface-cli download rogerai-fyi/DeepSeek-V4-Flash-MTP-GGUF
02

Run locally

Serve GGUF with llama.cpp as an OpenAI-compatible endpoint.

llama-server -m model.gguf --port 8080
03

Broadcast

Optional. Put that endpoint on air so others can tune in.

roger share
