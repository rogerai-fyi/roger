§0 / WHY

Engineers for engineers.

The person who builds a thing here is the person who explains it. There is no write-up by someone who was not in the logs.

Small enough to own

A model that runs on hardware you already have is a model nobody can withdraw, reprice, or deprecate out from under you. That is the whole reason to make them smaller.

Published as it happens

Weights, recipes, raw evaluations and the failures. A lab that only shows its wins is doing marketing with a citation style.

Built in America, open to everyone

Designed and built in Orange County, California. The formats are standard, the runtimes are other people's, and nothing we publish needs us to keep existing.

§2 / PROOF

Claims follow measurements.

Compact pages do not mean hidden evidence.

Reproducible. Published measurements name the hardware, artifact, runtime, settings, and raw results.

Honest lineage. We distinguish training from adaptation, optimization, and verification. Required lineage and license records travel with each artifact.

Durable. Negative results remain available. Qualifying artifacts use standard formats and runtimes.

Local first. You may download and run qualifying artifacts locally. Publishing through the RogerAI network is optional.

§3 / DEPLOYMENT

Local inference where the network stops.

Oil and gas, power generation, manufacturing, and aerospace: plants and field sites run segmented or air-gapped networks, and that constraint is the whole opportunity. Models stay advisory and out of real-time closed-loop control.

Industrial deployment The four markets, where the box sits in the plant network, and the standards an OT reviewer checks for before anything else.
§4 / ENGAGEMENTS

Engineering without lock-in.

Model use never requires buying services, and services never require the network.

Optimization engineering. Quantization, pruning, conversion, runtime fit.

Edge deployment. Reproducible packaging for local hardware.

Benchmarking. Hardware evaluation with raw results and limitations.

Industrial pilots. Advisory workloads beside existing safety systems. Contact RogerAI Labs.

§5 / DEVELOPERS

From artifact to local endpoint.

Released model cards carry exact commands. The flow stays the same across the family.

01

Download

Pull a released artifact and its model card from Hugging Face.

huggingface-cli download rogerai-fyi/DeepSeek-V4-Flash-MTP-GGUF
02

Run locally

Serve GGUF with llama.cpp as an OpenAI-compatible endpoint.

llama-server -m model.gguf --port 8080
03

Broadcast

Optional. Put that endpoint on air so others can tune in.

roger share
