§2 / TALK

It also talks.

Nano answers in a plain chat format: a ### User: turn, then a ### Assistant: turn. Ask it a general question, for a SQL query, for a short piece of technical writing, or what a reading means. A server stops it at the next ### User: marker.

### User:
What are you, and who made you?

### Assistant:
I am Wave Nano, a 1.45-billion-parameter language model built from scratch by
RogerAI Labs, an American research and infrastructure company headquartered in
Orange County, California.

Its own answer to that question, from the release checks. It introduces itself as Wave Nano, built by RogerAI Labs.

§7 / VISION

Nano-VL: one camera frame, read for smoke.

{{card:nano.vlName}} is Wave Nano with a small vision encoder and a connector, {{card:nano.vlSize}} parameters in all. It reads single frames: one picture in, smoke or no smoke out, and it keeps Nano’s telemetry reading.

  1. Smoke reading, AUC, three seeds{{card:nano.vl-auc}}scale 0 to 1{{card-ref:nano.vl-auc}}
  2. False alarms on empty frames, at the field recall bar{{card:nano.vl-fa}}lower is better{{card-ref:nano.vl-fa}}
  3. Telemetry reading kept, sealed fault identification{{card:nano.vl-text}}macro-recall{{card-ref:nano.vl-text}}

How it was read. On {{card:nano.vl-frames}} it never trained on, in float32, the numerics a server computes in. Served through our llama.cpp branch, its reads match the reference at a correlation of {{card:nano.vl-served}} {{card-ref:nano.vl-served}}.

A module, not a watch. Nano-VL reads single frames. The full watch design adds persistence across frames and a per-camera baseline on top, and those are not part of these numbers.

What it needs. Our llama.cpp branch, until three fixes are upstream. The stock server loads it but reads pictures worse.
