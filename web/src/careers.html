§4 / HOW TO APPLY

Send work, not a cover letter.

There is no applicant tracking system and no form. A person reads every message.

What to include. Anything that shows how you think: a repository, a paper, a build log, a bug you chased to the bottom. One paragraph on what you would want to own here is worth more than a page of history.

None of these fit? Send an open application anyway. Tell us what you are good at and what you would build, and if it is close to where we are going we will find the shape. careers@rogerai.fm

What we will tell you. Whether it is a fit, and why not if it is not. We are too small to leave people waiting, and being ignored by a company that publishes its failures would be an odd start.

What we cannot offer yet. We are early. Compensation is discussed honestly and individually rather than advertised as a band we have not set, and we will tell you plainly what the company is and is not before you decide anything.

§5 / WHY HERE

Built in America. Open to everyone.

The models, the runtimes and the evidence are published as they are made, because a lab that only shows its wins is not doing science. If you want your work read, argued with, and run on somebody's Raspberry Pi in a plant in Ohio, that is the job.
