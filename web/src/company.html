POSITION

Frontier capability, local footprint

Two directions, one goal: make frontier-capability models reachable on hardware you control, and make small models good enough that the frontier is not always the answer. Optimization work brings large open models down to workstation class; the Wave program builds up from the microcontroller.

Artifacts ship in open formats on standard runtimes, so nothing we publish depends on RogerAI continuing to exist.

§2 / FOCUS

The smallest model that can do the job.

We build for embedded and edge systems where operational-technology networks are segmented or air-gapped and data cannot leave.

A right-sized ladder. Roger Edge handles fixed signals and commands; Wave Micro targets sub-100M specialist work; Wave Nano is a 350M-class released model for text and tools; frontier-scale optimization brings upstream models with tens or hundreds of billions of parameters toward local hardware. The released checkpoint is wave-nano-350m-instruct; the other rungs keep their own research or design status.

Industrial deployment. Our focus spans oil and gas, power generation, manufacturing, and aerospace: advisory workloads beside existing control systems, never replacing deterministic protection or safety paths.

Evidence before claims. Measurements name the hardware, evaluation harness, settings, and raw results. Artifacts use open formats and standard runtimes; local inference sends no telemetry; negative results remain visible.

See the research notebook for programs, industry use cases, constraints, and device evidence.

§3 / ORIGIN & OPENNESS

Built here. Useful anywhere.

RogerAI is built in Orange County, California. “American-made” describes the company, product engineering, and RogerAI-authored research; it does not erase the upstream models and global research community this work depends on.

Open where the component is open. Our open-source model and runtime work, recipes, converters, evaluations, and qualifying weights are published with their actual licenses and lineage.

Source-visible is not the same claim. The RogerAI network and broker are licensed under PolyForm Perimeter 1.0.0. We publish the source, but do not call that component open source. Each artifact carries its own license rather than borrowing a broader marketing label.

Artifact license: Apache-2.0. The released Wave Nano v1.0 artifact may be used locally without the network. Optional broker publication, routing, payments, failover, and receipts are network services governed by separate network terms; those terms do not modify the artifact license.
