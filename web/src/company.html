§1 / WHAT WE BUILD

Two lines of work, one engineering discipline.

One line is a network that sends real work to machines other people own. The other is a family of small models built for the hardware those machines actually have.

POSITION

Frontier capability, local footprint

Two directions, one goal: make frontier-capability models reachable on hardware you control, and make small models good enough that the frontier is not always the answer. Optimization work brings large open models down to workstation class; the Wave program builds up from the single machine at the edge.

Our models ship in open formats on standard runtimes, so nothing we publish depends on RogerAI continuing to exist.

§2 / FOCUS

The smallest model that can do the job.

We build for embedded and edge systems where operational-technology networks are segmented or air-gapped and data cannot leave.

A right-sized family. The Wave Spectrum runs pico to exa on one SI scale. Every tier is named for where it sits.

  1. Wave Pico reads a single machine’s telemetry on hardware beside it.
  2. Wave Nano concentrates a fleet at the gateway.
  3. Wave Micro reasons across a facility,
  4. Wave Giga across a plant,
  5. and Wave Tera across many plants at once.
  6. Wave Peta carries regional scale, carved down from Wave Exa.
  7. Exa is the frontier flagship, and the teacher the rest of the family learns from.

Every tier ships with Wave Mesh baked in, and understands the output of every tier beneath it.

Industrial deployment. Our focus spans oil and gas, power generation, manufacturing, aerospace, mining, water, healthcare, and defense sustainment: advisory workloads beside existing control systems, never replacing deterministic protection or safety paths. In healthcare that means the equipment and facilities layer - never diagnosis, treatment, or a patient’s care.

Evidence before claims. Measurements name the hardware, evaluation harness, settings, and raw results. Our models use open formats and standard runtimes. Local inference sends no telemetry. Negative results remain visible.

§3 / ORIGIN & OPENNESS

Built here. Useful anywhere.

RogerAI is built in Orange County, California. “American-made” describes the company, product engineering, and RogerAI-authored research, built on upstream open models and a global research community.

Open where the component is open. Our open-source model and runtime work, recipes, converters, evaluations, and qualifying weights are published with their actual licenses and lineage.

Source-visible is not the same claim. The RogerAI network and broker are licensed under PolyForm Perimeter 1.0.0, and the node-agent protocol and usage-receipt SDK are Apache 2.0 so anyone can implement them. We publish the source, but do not call the platform open source. Each model carries its own license rather than borrowing a broader marketing label.

Model license. A released Wave model will carry its own license, and may be used locally without the network. Optional broker publication, routing, payments, failover, and receipts are network services governed by separate network terms. Those terms do not modify the model license.
