The Wave Spectrum: one family, pico to exa.

One family of industrial models on a single SI scale - from a chip beside the machine to an exascale-class flagship. Smaller waves reach the edge; larger waves carry more power. Each tier has a different job, and a measurement decides which job it is allowed to take.

§1 / SCALE

The gaps are real.

Tiers sit at their target artifact size on a logarithmic axis, so the spacing carries information instead of decorating the page. Wave Exa is roughly a thousand times a Wave Pico.

§2 / CAPABILITY

Ninety-three percent deleted. The work order still comes out right.

Sixty experts kept out of 896, and the plant’s real jobs run against what was left.

That boundary is the product. Extraction, classification and tool use survive heavy compression, so they belong in the small slots. Explanation and multi-step reasoning do not, so they belong in the large ones. It is why a single-digit-billion Wave Micro is enough for a work order and why Wave Giga exists for the rest.

Specialists first. Wave Pico and Wave Nano are task-native total specialists. By design they score at chance on general benchmarks (MMLU 26.9 against a 25 chance line, Pico v4 audit). Reasoning and explanation grow from Wave Micro upward, where every tier must stay competitive on general benchmarks as well as industrial ones.

How the recipes split. Scratch means trained from random init on our data, and it is bounded by diverse tokens, not GPUs - measured healthy with no starvation knee through 1.5 billion parameters. Pico and Nano are the scratch tiers. From Micro up, base + specialize: a strong open base, industrial continued-pretrain, and the mesh. Peta and Exa are carved from the flagship rather than trained. Sizes are targets and bands, not final specifications.

One family on one wire. Every tier ships with Wave Mesh baked in and understands the output of every tier beneath it.

§6 / WAVE INFINITE

The model that grows.

Prototype · measured foundations, growth loop in development

Every slot above is a fixed size. Wave Infinite is a prototype model program. Start from a Nano, Micro, or Giga base, and the model grows new weights: real parameters, trained on your work, not prompts and not retrieval. All of it happens inside a proof that the base behavior is preserved.

TRAINING IT IS USING IT
  1. Start. Pick a base: Nano, Micro, or Giga. Infinite is the loop, not another tier.
  2. Work. The model observes its own execution and grows new weights only where measured utility says they help.
  3. Certify. Changes go live through the same certified path, with an exact rollback always resident. If the work shifts beyond what is certified, it reverts to the original path by itself.
WHAT WE HAVE PROVEN
The certificate is sound
Removing every expert it marked unreachable left the model’s routing bit-identical - not close, unchanged.
Specialization is safe on stable work
Within a single domain the guard almost never fires, so the specialization holds for a stable workload.
The guard is necessary
The moment the workload changes domain, the same certificates would miss a large share of decisions. The fallback is what makes aggression safe.
Standard pruning over-removes
Frequency analysis nominated an order of magnitude more for removal than could be proven safe, across three model architectures.

Measured on interim hardware; figures publish after final-hardware re-verification.

IN MEASUREMENT

End-to-end speed of certified specialization, and the growth layer’s quality gains. The prototype program publishes both with error bars.

What it requires. The certificate is reachability certification of mixture-of-experts routing. It applies only to a model that has experts and a per-expert selection bias to read. That is a base-model choice rather than something the family has today. It does not reach the microcontroller-class edge at all: a classifier of a few hundred kilobytes is not a language model.
