← RogerAI Labs ((•)) WAVE FAMILY · FIELD GUIDE

One family, seven orders of magnitude.

From a model that fits on a microcontroller to one that fills a workstation. Each slot has a different job, and a measurement decides which job it is allowed to take.

§1 / SCALE

The gaps are real.

Slots sit at their true artifact size on a logarithmic axis, so the spacing carries information instead of decorating the page. Wave Micro is roughly a hundred times a Roger Edge model.

§2 / CAPABILITY

Compression takes explanation first.

We cut a frontier mixture-of-experts model to 6.7 percent of its experts and measured what broke. The loss was not uniform, and the pattern is what assigns jobs to slots.

What it means for the family. Extraction, classification, and tool use belong in the small slots. Explanation, synthesis, and multi-step reasoning belong in the large ones. A slot that ignores this ships a model that sounds confident and is wrong, which in a plant is worse than one that declines.

Controlled. The same prompts were run against the uncompressed model, which answered every one, including the pump question. The prompts are answerable and the harness terminates, so the failure belongs to the compression.

Scope. This measures whether an answer arrives, not whether it is right, and it compares two points rather than a curve. Where on the compression path explanation gives way is not yet measured.

What each size is for.

Program status travels with each slot. No slot has released an artifact yet.

Not chat models. Roger Edge, Wave Nano, and Wave Micro extract, classify, route, and call tools. Marketing them as small assistants would contradict the measurement in section 2.

Where explanation starts. Wave Core is the first slot whose headline job is explaining rather than extracting, and the first allowed to claim it.

Why the family stops. Above the Station class you are buying knowledge breadth, which is rarely the binding constraint for a disconnected site.

Guard is the one buyers ask about first. Section 2 says small slots will be wrong when pushed past extraction. Guard is what turns that from dangerous into safe, which is why it spans every slot rather than arriving late.
