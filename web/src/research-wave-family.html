One family, seven orders of magnitude.

From a model that fits on a microcontroller to one that fills a workstation. Each slot has a different job, and a measurement decides which job it is allowed to take.

§1 / SCALE

The gaps are real.

Slots sit at their true artifact size on a logarithmic axis, so the spacing carries information instead of decorating the page. Wave Micro is roughly a hundred times a Roger Edge model.

§2 / CAPABILITY

Cut to 6.7 percent, it still files the work order.

We deleted 93.3 percent of a frontier mixture-of-experts model and ran the plant’s real jobs against what was left.

That boundary is the product. Extraction, classification and tool use survive heavy compression, so they belong in the small slots. Explanation and multi-step reasoning do not, so they belong in the large ones. It is why a 350M model is enough for a work order and why Wave Core exists for the rest.

Controlled. The same prompts ran against the uncompressed model, which answered every one, including the pump question - so the failure belongs to the compression, not the prompts. Scope. This measures whether an answer arrives, not whether it is right, and it compares two points rather than a curve. Where on the compression path explanation gives way is not yet measured.

Not chat models. Roger Edge, Wave Nano, and Wave Micro extract, classify, route, and call tools. Marketing them as small assistants would contradict the measurement in section 2.

Where explanation starts. Wave Core is the first slot whose headline job is explaining rather than extracting, and the first allowed to claim it.

Why the family stops. Above the Station class you are buying knowledge breadth, which is rarely the binding constraint for a disconnected site.

§6 / WAVE INFINITE

A runtime, not a size.

Everything above is a model. Wave Infinite is the layer any of them could run under - a runtime that applies across the family rather than sitting at a point in it. It watches its own execution, proves which parts of the model your workload can never reach, and skips those parts. Every modification carries a machine-checkable certificate that behaviour is unchanged on the region observed, and any input leaving that region falls back to the unmodified path.

PROVEN

The certificate is causally sound, and the guard is required: 44 to 96 percent of certified experts fire once the workload shifts.

NOT PROVEN

Soundness is proven; speedup is unmeasured. No performance benefit has been demonstrated. Stages 3 and 4 are not built, and the self-improvement half is entirely unmeasured and out of scope. Until a realised tokens-per-second number exists, this is a research architecture.

THE THREE WORDS

Reflecting - yes, in the procedural sense: it acts on its own execution state. Evolving - half: the deployment evolves, the model does not. Learning - no. Nothing learns, no weights change, and the weights on disk after a year are the ones you installed.

About the name. The reflective-tower literature proved a notionally infinite tower of self-observation has a finite implementation, because only finitely many levels are ever touched. Infinite in specification, finite in implementation - a theorem, not a capability claim.
