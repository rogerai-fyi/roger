One family, seven orders of magnitude.

From a model that fits on a microcontroller to one that fills a workstation. Each slot has a different job, and a measurement decides which job it is allowed to take.

§1 / SCALE

The gaps are real.

Slots sit at their true artifact size on a logarithmic axis, so the spacing carries information instead of decorating the page. Wave Micro is roughly a hundred times a Roger Edge model.

§2 / CAPABILITY

Ninety-three percent deleted. The work order still comes out right.

Sixty experts kept out of 896, and the plant’s real jobs run against what was left.

That boundary is the product. Extraction, classification and tool use survive heavy compression, so they belong in the small slots. Explanation and multi-step reasoning do not, so they belong in the large ones. It is why a 350M model is enough for a work order and why Wave Core exists for the rest.

Failing to answer is the failure worth having. The zero is not ten confident errors, it is ten non-answers - and in a plant those are not the same event. A model that invents a torque spec is dangerous; one that does not answer is merely limited, and the limit is knowable in advance. That is why Guard, the variation that declines and escalates, spans every slot rather than arriving late.

Controlled. The same prompts ran against the uncompressed model, which answered every one, including the pump question - so the failure belongs to the compression, not the prompts. Scope. This measures whether an answer arrives, not whether it is right, and it compares two points rather than a curve. Where on the compression path explanation gives way is not yet measured.

Not chat models. Roger Edge, Wave Nano, and Wave Micro extract, classify, route, and call tools. Marketing them as small assistants would contradict the measurement in section 2.

Where explanation starts. Wave Core is the first slot whose headline job is explaining rather than extracting, and the first allowed to claim it.

Why the family stops. Above the Station class you are buying knowledge breadth, which is rarely the binding constraint for a disconnected site.

Guard is the one buyers ask about first. Section 2 says small slots will be wrong when pushed past extraction. Guard is what turns that from dangerous into safe, which is why it spans every slot rather than arriving late.

§6 / WAVE INFINITE

Specialisation with a proof, not a promise.

Everything above is a model. Wave Infinite is a prototype model programme: a model that adapts to your work and can prove what it did not change. It is built in three layers, and they are at three different states of proof.

WHAT WE HAVE PROVEN
The certificate is sound
Removing every expert it marked unreachable left the model’s routing bit-identical - not close, unchanged.
Specialisation is safe on stable work
Within a single domain the guard almost never fires, so the specialisation holds for a stable workload.
The guard is necessary
The moment the workload changes domain, the same certificates would miss a large share of decisions. The fallback is what makes aggression safe.
Standard pruning over-removes
Frequency analysis nominated an order of magnitude more for removal than could be proven safe, across three model architectures.

All four are measurements rather than projections. The figures behind them were taken on interim hardware and are published once they have been re-verified on the final hardware.

WHAT WE HAVE NOT PROVEN

We publish this line deliberately. The end-to-end speed benefit of certified specialisation is unmeasured, and so are the growth layer’s quality gains. Both are in measurement now.

Wave Infinite is a prototype programme. When those numbers exist they will be published with error bars, like everything above.

What it requires. The certificate is reachability certification of mixture-of-experts routing, so it applies to a model that has experts and a per-expert selection bias to read. That is a base-model choice rather than something the family has today, and it does not reach Roger Edge at all - that is a classifier of a few hundred kilobytes, not a language model.
