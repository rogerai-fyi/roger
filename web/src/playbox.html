Open the console.

Load a model like a cassette, choose what goes in - text, your voice, an image, a tool request, an embedded signal, or a guarded command - and press play. LIVE tapes are hosted models answering over the network. The Wave shelf also carries clearly marked recorded contracts, trained-but-off-air weights, and planned bands, so you can see what exists without being told it is hosted when it is not.

RogerAI builds LLMs for machines. Wire a device to a model, play the signal, and watch what it asserts. Every device, channel and wire format on this deck is a committed export of the simulation suite the models are actually trained and measured against - not an illustration of one.

A cookie line: mixer, oven, packager. Each machine has one sensor and one dial, and you start with no models - so a sensor that quietly goes bad keeps showing a comfortable number while your line dies behind it. Every Wave model you add takes a piece of that work off you - until the line runs itself.
