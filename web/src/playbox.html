    How it routes

    Tuning a station here is the real thing: your message goes through the Tower’s relay (POST /v1/chat/completions, credentialed to this site only) to whoever is sharing that model, and the reply streams back co-signed and metered - the same path the CLI uses, on the same wallet. Free stations work signed out; paid stations ask you to sign in first.

    With no station tuned, you’re talking to Ping, the concierge. When the band is quiet or a station drops, the page says so - nothing here is ever simulated.

    Roger Edge: input in, structured decision out.

    Roger Edge and Wave Nano aren’t chat models - they turn a messy device input into a structured decision: a JSON extraction, a tool call, or an ESCALATE. The screen shows the certified contract for that input - the target keyed to a published standard - and, beside it, what a captured preview checkpoint actually returned. The gap is the work.
