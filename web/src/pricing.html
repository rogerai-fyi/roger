There is no price list. There are rules.

Every station on this network is somebody's machine, and the person who owns it sets what it charges. Plenty of them charge nothing. Rates here are set by the operator, never by us, so the number you pay is not ours to publish - it is live on the dial and it moves. What does not move is the part below: how you are billed, what you can be billed, and what an operator keeps.

§2 / WHAT YOU PAY

Per token, from a wallet, at a price the operator set.

The whole billing model in one plate. None of these are introductory and none of them change when you spend more.

Unit
Per token, input and output priced separately, quoted in dollars per million. Voices are billed by the character.
Who sets it
The operator, per model, and they can vary it by time of day. We do not set, subsidize, or floor it. The current number for any band is on the dial.
Subscription
None. No plan, no seat, no monthly minimum, no annual commitment, and nothing to cancel. If you stop sending requests you stop spending.
Wallet
Debited live as tokens settle. The debit is conditional on the broker side, so a wallet cannot go negative - there is no bill at the end of the month and no overage to argue about.
To start
A $1 starter credit lands on your wallet the first time you log in, one time per account, so you can try a paid station before you ever add money. It is a launch seed and it is capped, so it lasts while it lasts.
Top-ups
Through Stripe Checkout, in whatever amount you choose: roger topup 25. Stripe is the payments provider and holds every card detail; we never see one.
An unusable answer
Costs nothing. A request that returns nothing usable is voided: the pre-authorized hold is released, you pay zero, the operator earns zero and takes a strike. A model that cannot actually answer never appears on your bill.
The token count
Is the broker's own re-count, not the station's claim, and settlement takes the smaller of the two. Padding the count is not a thing a station can profitably do.
§3 / PRICE SAFETY

An open market needs a guard rail on the expensive side.

Anyone can list a station, which means anyone can list an absurd one. Four rules stand between a typo - yours or theirs - and your balance.

A ceiling you did not have to set

Set no limit and the client still caps every connection at $10 / 1M out. That floor holds even under --yes, so an unattended bot can never quietly bind itself to an expensive band.

Type the price back

To accept a station above $20 / 1M out you have to type the number, not press y. A deliberate speed bump on the genuinely costly end of the dial.

A listing ceiling

The broker refuses to register a station above $100 / 1M out or $50 / 1M in, base rate and every scheduled window. It binds every band, public or private: a private band hides a station from the market, it is not a way around the ceiling.

No price hike, ever

Your ceiling is not a one-time check at connect. It rides every request as a header, so a station cannot raise its rate under you mid-session. If a band drifts past your limit you get routed elsewhere, not overcharged.

§4 / WHAT YOU EARN

You keep 70 percent, and you set the number it is 70 percent of.

The other side of the same market. Nothing here is negotiated, tiered, or different for a large operator.

Your share
70% of every token you serve. The 30% platform fee covers routing, metering, settlement, fraud and chargeback exposure, and the payout rails.
Through a Tower
When a relay carries the work, the settlement is 70 / 10 / 20: the serving node, the Tower operator, then the platform. How that path works.
You control
Input rate, output rate, free hours, time-of-use windows, concurrency, and which models you carry. Setting a rate of zero is a normal thing to do and costs you nothing.
Minimum payout
$25. Below that the transfer and reporting overhead is worth more than the transfer.
Schedule
You request it; payable balances are released on a monthly batch.
Identity
Stripe Connect onboarding has to be complete before any payout leaves. Stripe holds the identity documents - we never store them. Earnings still accrue before you onboard; they are simply not transferable yet.
AccruedA served request credits your share the moment it settles.
HeldFor 120 days, which covers the late-chargeback tail. There is no separate reserve on top of it.
PayableHold cleared, no open dispute, Connect onboarding done.
PaidYou request a payout and Stripe transfers it to your bank.
§5 / WHAT IS NOT BILLED

The list of things nobody is charging you for.

  • Reading the dial. The whole live directory, every station and every price, with no account.
  • The software. The CLI, the terminal app, the iOS and Mac app, and the source. There is no paid edition.
  • Serving for free. Running a station at a rate of zero costs nothing and is not rate-limited into uselessness.
  • A standalone Tower. Your own relay, your own machines, your own network, with no account and nothing reported anywhere.
  • A private band. Handing your own people a key to your own station. How grants work.
  • An answer that did not work. Voided, not discounted.
