The wildfire watch, as designed.

Warning and mitigation, never fire protection. In this design the models warn and explain; the model never actuates equipment.

The design. Wave Pico would sit on the mast, reading particulates, carbon monoxide, volatile organics, thermal, the camera module’s outputs and the sprinkler controller’s state. Wave Nano would sit on an edge box with a camera smoke detector and read the whole site. A larger tier would weigh all of it.

What is measured, and where. The routed fire adapters and the fire-site rollup are measured in our simulator, so every fire number on this page is simulator-internal. False-alarm rates at a real site over months are not measured yet, and nothing here is a claim about suppression.

Pre-wet is the owner’s rule. The owner arms it in advance. The controller, not the model, executes that owner-armed rule, with its own abort window, and only after the escalation ladder has been tried and no person acknowledged. The model’s part is to mark the site pre-wet eligible; it never opens a valve.

This is not a fire alarm or a sprinkler system, and it is never in the control path of one. It does not replace code-required detection or suppression, or the fire service.
